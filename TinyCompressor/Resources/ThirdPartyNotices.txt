Tiny Compressor bundles these open-source command-line tools for image compression:

- pngquant 3.0.3: GPL-3.0-or-later AND HPND AND BSD-2-Clause
  Source: https://github.com/kornelski/pngquant
- Zopfli 1.0.3: Apache-2.0
  Source: https://github.com/google/zopfli
- OxiPNG 10.1.1: MIT
  Source: https://github.com/oxipng/oxipng
- MozJPEG 4.1.5: IJG AND Zlib AND BSD-3-Clause
  Source: https://github.com/mozilla/mozjpeg
- jpegoptim 1.5.6: GPL-3.0-or-later
  Source: https://github.com/tjko/jpegoptim
- libwebp 1.6.0: BSD-3-Clause
  Source: https://chromium.googlesource.com/webm/libwebp
- Little CMS 2.18: MIT
  Source: https://www.littlecms.com/
- libpng 1.6.58: libpng-2.0
  Source: https://github.com/pnggroup/libpng
- libjpeg-turbo 3.1.4.1: IJG AND Zlib AND BSD-3-Clause
  Source: https://github.com/libjpeg-turbo/libjpeg-turbo
